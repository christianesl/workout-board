Session rule

Easy pace · talk test · no sprint
5 km continuous · about 6:24 / km · 32 min
Today’s target is consistency, not speed. Stay at a comfortable pace you could hold a short sentence through. If breathing gets choppy, slow down. Arms stay low and relaxed. No overhead reach, no aggressive arm drive, shoulder blades packed and stable.

Warm-up · 8 min

1
Easy walk
2 min · nose or easy mouth breathing · upright posture
2
Small arm circles / march
1 min · circles stay below shoulder height · blades packed
3
Leg swings
1 min · front-to-back, then side-to-side · hold a wall, not the shoulder
4
Walking lunges
1 min · short steps · hands on hips, no load
5
Easy jog build
3 min · walk to jog, then settle into today’s pace

Main run · 32 min

1
Km 1 · settle
~6:30–7:00 / km · slower than you think
Find cadence. Quiet feet. Shoulders down. If the first minute feels hard, you started too fast.
2
Km 2–4 · lock pace
~6:20–6:30 / km · same effort the whole way
One gear only. Match each kilometer. Breathing stays conversational. No surges on hills — shorten the stride.
3
Km 5 · finish smooth
Hold the same pace · no kick
Last 200 m stays controlled. Stop the watch when you hit 5.00 km, then walk.
Walk 20–30 s only if needed · then resume easy

Cool-down · 5 min

1
Walk it down
2 min · hands relaxed at your sides
2
Calf + quad
1 min · wall calf stretch, then standing quad · 20 s each side
3
Hip flexor + hamstring
1 min · half-kneel hip flexor, then hinge hamstring · no arm reach overhead
4
Shoulder reset
1 min · scapular squeezes and slow shoulder rolls, range only where it is pain-free
